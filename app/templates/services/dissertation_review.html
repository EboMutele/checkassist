Is it allowed to get feedback on my dissertation?

Most universities allow proofreading and feedback that doesn't change your content, but the rules vary and some ask you to declare it. Check your university's proofreading policy. We never write or rewrite content, which is the line most policies draw.

Can you check formatting against my university's guide?

Yes, if you tell us which rules apply: margins, heading levels, numbering, captions and referencing style. Paste the key rules in the instructions box when you upload.

Do you review master's and PhD work?

Yes: undergraduate dissertations, master's theses, PhD chapters and research proposals. Your reviewer reads for structure, argument and clarity, not for specialist accuracy in your field.

Will my reviewer write or rewrite sections?

No. We point out what isn't working and why, and you make the changes. That's what your supervisor and examiners expect, and it keeps the work yours.
