Feedback, not a rewrite

We won't rewrite your work or change it so that it scores differently. You make the changes, using our notes. That's what most universities allow, and it's what holds up if you're asked to talk about your work.

A detector's score is a probability, not proof, and a similarity score isn't a plagiarism verdict. Our job is to tell you what's really behind yours. More in what to do if you're flagged.

How do I send the report?

Upload your document, then add the report as a screenshot or PDF in the conversation on your document's page. No report yet? Ask for an AI detection and plagiarism report and we'll start from that.

My own work was flagged. Can you prove it's mine?

No one can prove that from the text alone, and a detector's score isn't proof either. What we can do is explain each flag and point you to the evidence that helps: your drafts, version history, notes and sources.

Will you rewrite the flagged parts for me?

No. We show you what was flagged, why, and what to fix. The changes are yours to make.

Is my document kept private?

It's kept privately in your account, and only you and our team can open it. It's never added to a plagiarism database, never shared and never used to train AI.
