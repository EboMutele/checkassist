Proofreading or editing?

Proofreading fixes the surface: grammar, spelling, punctuation and consistency. Editing questions what's underneath: whether the argument holds, the structure works and the evidence fits. Most services charge for each separately.

Every check here includes both. If you only want a proofread, say so in the instructions box and your reviewer will stick to the surface. If you want a full essay review, you're already covered.

Do you use UK or US English?

Whichever you tell us. If you don't say, we follow the spelling most of your document already uses and make the rest match.

How is this different from a grammar checker?

Software flags patterns. A reviewer reads for meaning, so they catch the right word in the wrong place, sentences that are grammatical but unclear, and points that contradict each other three pages apart.
