Will you write my personal statement?

No. It has to sound like you, and admissions teams look for that. We show you what to cut, what to expand and what's unclear, and you rewrite it.

Can you check it against the character limit?

Yes. Tell us the limit and whether it counts spaces, and we'll say how far over or under you are and what to cut first.

Can I send more than one draft?

Yes. Each upload is read in full and uses one check, so many applicants buy a pack and send each draft as they go.

Do you know what my university or scholarship wants?

Paste the prompt and any guidance from the application into the instructions box. Your reviewer checks your statement against what they actually ask for.
