Claims that need a source
Statistics, dates, definitions and phrases like “research shows” or “historians agree”. Anything your reader would ask “says who?” about.
Quotes and paraphrases
Direct quotes need a page or paragraph number in most styles. Close paraphrases need a citation even without quotation marks.
Citations against your reference list
Every in-text citation should appear in your list, with the same spelling and year, and every entry in the list should be cited somewhere.
Style
Author names, dates, italics, punctuation and order, checked against the style you name: APA 7, MLA 9, Harvard or Chicago.
Entries that look wrong
References with missing details, or that we can't trace, flagged so you can check them against the source before you submit.

Is this a plagiarism check?

Not on its own. If you want a similarity score and an AI score as well, see plagiarism and AI reports. A citation check is about what's behind those numbers.

What we do catch is the citation gaps that most often lead to accidental plagiarism: ideas taken from a source without credit, quotes without page numbers, and paraphrases that stay too close to the original. Fix those, and every borrowed idea in your paper is credited.

Which citation styles do you check?

APA 7, MLA 9, Harvard and Chicago, plus others if you tell us which guide your course uses. Harvard varies between universities, so if yours has its own guide, paste the key rules in the instructions box.

Will you fix my reference list?

We mark each formatting error in your copy and show the correct form. Where a detail is missing, like a page range or a DOI, we flag it: you have the source, so you're the one who can look it up.

Will this make sure my paper passes Turnitin?

No one can promise a similarity score. A score isn't the same as plagiarism anyway: markers read the report to see whether matched text is properly quoted and credited. That's exactly what we check. More in Turnitin similarity scores, explained.

Do you only check citations, or the writing too?

Both, if you want. Every check includes notes on argument and clarity and a marked-up copy. If you only want citations looked at, say so when you upload.
