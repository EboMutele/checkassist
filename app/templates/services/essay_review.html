Does it answer the question?
We read your title or prompt first, then check that every section works toward it. Where the essay drifts, we show you the paragraph and the sentence where it happens.
Thesis and structure
Is your main point clear by the end of the introduction? Does each paragraph open with its job and link to the next? We mark where the order could be tighter.
Evidence
Claims that need a source, quotes that are left to speak for themselves, and examples that don't support the point you use them for.
Clarity and grammar
Long sentences, vague wording, grammar and spelling, marked in your copy so you can take or leave each edit.
The brief
The word limit, the citation style and any formatting rules you give us, checked against the whole essay.

Will you correct my essay for me?

We mark grammar and spelling corrections in your copy, and explain bigger changes in your notes. We don't rewrite your argument or add content. You make those changes, so the essay stays yours.

What kinds of essays do you review?

Coursework essays, argumentative and discursive essays, reflective essays, literature reviews and short reports, at school, college and university level. If it's a dissertation chapter, see dissertation review; if it's an application, see personal statement review.

Can I send a draft that isn't finished?

Yes. Say it's a draft and what you want checked, and your reviewer will focus there. Early feedback is often the most useful, because there's still time to change the structure.
