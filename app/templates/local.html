Questions

How do I pay with M-Pesa?

After you choose a pack, Paystack's checkout opens. Pick M-Pesa, enter your number, and approve the prompt that appears on your phone with your M-Pesa PIN. Your checks appear as soon as the payment is confirmed.

Is it the same service as the main site?

Yes: the same reviewers and the same notes and marked-up copy. Only the prices and the ways to pay are different.

Will you write or rewrite my work?

No. We show you what to fix and why, and you make the changes, so the work stays yours. How we review
