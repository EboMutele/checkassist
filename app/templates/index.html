Questions

Who reads my paper?

A reviewer on our team reads the whole document, start to finish. Your notes are written by that person, not generated.

Is my paper private?

Your file is stored privately so your reviewer can read it. It's never added to any database, never shared, and never used to train AI. Only you and our review team can open it.

Will you rewrite it for me?

No. Your reviewer shows you what to change and why, and you make the changes. The work stays yours.
